Board, ride and find your stop

🚉 AREX all-stop

Go to the Airport Railroad station at the terminal transport center. Confirm the current adult fare and payment rules with AREX. For hotels beyond an AREX station, check your additional subway, taxi or walking route and cost.

🚄 AREX Express

Purchase a separate reserved-seat ticket. This service runs to Seoul Station only; transfers for other destinations are not included in the separate train ticket. Confirm the scheduled departure before paying.

🚌 Airport limousine bus

Choose a bus serving your hotel area, confirm the exact stop and terminal boarding bay with the operator, then use the walking-map link to inspect the final part of your trip. Do not assume a suggested area-level stop is the nearest stop. We avoid displaying unverified fares and do not assume a bus stops at your hotel.

🚕 Licensed taxi

Follow the official taxi signs. Metered fares depend on actual distance, tolls, traffic and surcharges. Confirm vehicle capacity for your party and luggage; special international taxi zone products are not ordinary meter quotes.
